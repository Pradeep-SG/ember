# Welcome message — Kindrith Forge

*Post this as a pinned message in `#welcome`, and DM it to each new member on join.*

---

**Welcome to Kindrith Forge.**

You're here because you have a habit you want to beat — a cigarette, a late-night scroll, a drink, a binge — and you're willing to help us build a tool that might make that fight different.

**What Kindrith is.** A single-player game where your real-world discipline powers a small magical realm. Every habit you keep fuels a hearth at its center; every craving you feel summons a Shadow Demon you fight in a short tactical mini-game. The idea: when a craving hits, the app becomes *more engaging than the thing the craving wants*. That's the bet.

**What Kindrith is not.** Not a treatment. Not a substitute for a therapist, a doctor, or a support group. If you are in acute crisis, please talk to someone who can help — resources in `#rules`.

**What you're here to do.**

1. **Join a 2-week diary study.** When you get a craving, you choose whether to open the app. You log what happened — either way. We don't judge. The data is what teaches us what works.
2. **Play the TestFlight build** when it's ready (expected ~Week 4–6). iOS only for now.
3. **Tell us what's wrong.** Every way the app misses — the timing feels off, the demon's voice is wrong, the breathing circle is confusing — we need to hear it before launch. Bluntness is a gift here.
4. **Tell us what works.** When something lands — a specific line from the Demon that hit, a battle you didn't expect to win — we need that too. It teaches us which decisions to protect.

**How we'll ask things of you.**

- Short prompts in `#craving-check-ins` — answer if you feel like it.
- One voice-call conversation during the study (~30 min) — optional but we value it.
- A post-study survey.

**Pace and privacy.**

- Quiet days are fine. Lurking is fine. The app will still work for you even if you never post.
- What you write in `#craving-check-ins` is visible to other members. Use as much or as little detail as you're comfortable with — "urge at 9pm, didn't open" is a complete entry.
- We don't screenshot or repost anything outside this server without your explicit okay.

**Who you're talking to.** Pradeep — the designer. I'll be in the server most days. If I'm slow to respond, I'm not ignoring you, I'm building.

Pinned reading when you have a minute:

- `#rules` — how we behave here.
- `#pre-commitment` — what you're agreeing to by staying.

Introduce yourself in `#general` when you're ready. One line is fine.

🔥 Welcome to the Forge.

— *Pradeep & Kindrith*
