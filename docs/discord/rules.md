# Kindrith Forge — House rules

*Post in `#rules` and enforce gently. Pinned.*

---

### 1. Kindness first.

Everyone here is fighting something. Nobody needs a lecture. Celebrate each other's wins — however small. Say nothing when you would shame.

### 2. Your story is yours.

What someone shares in `#craving-check-ins` stays in `#craving-check-ins`. No screenshots, no cross-posting to other servers, no reposting on social. Breaking this is a ban, not a warning.

### 3. No medical advice.

Kindrith is a wellness tool. It is not a treatment. Do not give medical advice to other members, and do not treat advice you receive here as medical. If you're prescribed medication, keep taking it. If you're in a program, keep going.

### 4. No selling.

No product links, no affiliate codes, no supplement pitches, no coaching services, no recruiting. If you're not sure, ask before posting.

### 5. NSFW, substances beyond the study scope, and violence stay out.

We're focused on habit change. The content here reflects that. Discussion of the specific habit you're trying to beat is welcome. Pushing others toward substances, romanticizing them, or unrelated extreme content — out.

### 6. No AI-generated journal entries.

We want your actual experience, in your words, even if those words are short. Using an LLM to generate diary entries pollutes the signal we need to build a thing that actually works.

### 7. Relapses are information.

If you slip, you are welcome to say so here. We are *absolutely not* a server that shames lapses. The single best predictor of full relapse is shame about a lapse. We refuse to be that for each other.

### 8. Crisis support.

We are not crisis responders. If you are having thoughts of harming yourself or others, please use one of these:

- **US/Canada:** 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text)
- **UK/Ireland:** Samaritans — 116 123
- **Australia:** Lifeline — 13 11 14
- **International:** [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com)

A moderator will quietly reach out with these resources if we see a post that concerns us. Please don't take that as judgment — it's how we look out for each other.

### 9. Bugs go in `#bug-reports`, features in `#feedback`, check-ins in `#craving-check-ins`.

Use the right channel. We read all of them.

### 10. One warning, then a day-long mute, then a ban.

We'd rather talk it out. If we can't, we'll act clearly.

---

*Rules update as we learn. Changes will be announced in `#general` before they take effect.*
