# What you're signing up for

*Pinned in `#pre-commitment` and linked from the welcome DM. Read once, decide if you're in.*

---

**Plain version.** You're agreeing to help us test a habit-change app over about six weeks. There are four things we'll ask of you.

---

### 1. A 2-week craving diary (starts before the app is built)

Starting roughly **Week 2 of your time in the server**, we'll ask you to keep a light diary. Every time you notice a craving for the habit you're trying to beat — a cigarette, a scroll, a drink, whatever — you log one entry. It can be 10 seconds:

- Roughly when it hit
- What you were doing
- How intense it felt (1–5)
- What you did (gave in, rode it out, did something else — anything)

That's it. The diary captures your baseline — how cravings show up in your life *before* Kindrith has any say. Later we compare it to the diary kept *with* the app. Your baseline is as valuable to us as anything else.

You can log in a notes app, a Google Doc, a piece of paper — whatever you'll actually do. We'll give you a simple template, but the form doesn't matter.

**Time cost:** ~10 sec per entry, a few entries per day, for 2 weeks.

---

### 2. A TestFlight build of the Shadow Battle prototype (~Week 4–6)

When the first playable prototype is ready, we'll send you a TestFlight invite. iOS only for now. The prototype is just one mini-game — the Shadow Battle. No full app yet. Programmer art.

We'll ask you to:

- **Install within 48 hours** of the invite (TestFlight invites expire and it helps us keep the cohort tight).
- **When a craving hits, consider opening the app.** You don't have to every time. We want to know how often you remember, and what happens when you do.
- **After each battle, the app asks one or two questions.** 20 seconds. Skip them if you want to — we'd rather have honest skipping than pressured answers.

**Time cost:** 3–5 minutes per battle session; you choose how often.

---

### 3. One voice call with Pradeep during the study (~30 min)

Sometime in your second week with the prototype, we'd love a 30-minute call. Voice or video, your pick. We'll ask open questions:

- When did you remember to open the app? When didn't you?
- What about the battle worked? What felt off?
- Did the Demon ever say something that landed? Something that missed?

This is the single most valuable part of the study for us. Surveys miss nuance; a conversation doesn't. Optional but we strongly prefer it.

**Time cost:** 30 minutes.

---

### 4. A 10-minute post-study survey

At the end of the 2-week prototype period, a short survey. Multiple-choice and one free-text at the end. We share a redacted summary back to the server when we publish our findings.

**Time cost:** 10 minutes.

---

## What we give in exchange

- **Honest behind-the-scenes.** We show our homework. When we change the app based on your feedback, we say so and credit the observation.
- **Permanent "Early Forge" status.** Once we launch, you keep a cosmetic marker in-app that marks you as a founding playtester. Doesn't do anything mechanical; it exists.
- **Free Kindrith+ for life.** When we add a paid tier, you never pay for it.
- **Named thanks in credits.** Opt-in. We'll ask.

---

## What we don't do

- **We don't pay you.** We thought about a stipend and decided against it — paid testers subtly bend their answers toward what they think we want. We'd rather have honest people who aren't in it for the money.
- **We don't sell your data.** Ever. Not now, not later.
- **We don't keep you on a list after the study.** If you leave, we delete anything tied to you (we'll email to confirm).
- **We don't ask you to recruit friends** without telling you explicitly.

---

## Who this is not right for

- You're in acute crisis right now. Please talk to someone trained — resources in `#rules`. Come back to this study when you have ground under you.
- You don't have the habit you're testing against. We need real cravings to learn from.
- You can't fit ~15 minutes of commitment per week for six weeks.

---

## Opting in

You're opted in the moment you post an introduction in `#general`. If you change your mind, you can opt out any time — no explanation needed. Just DM Pradeep, or post "bowing out" in `#general`.

---

**Bottom line:** about 15 minutes of your time per week for six weeks, one voice call, a TestFlight build, honest feedback. In exchange: the chance to shape an app that might actually help someone fight the thing you're fighting.

🔥 If that's a deal, introduce yourself in `#general`.
