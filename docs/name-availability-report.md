# Name availability report — "Ember"

> **Decision recorded 2026-04-24: renamed to Kindrith.** After this report recommended rename, four candidates were screened (Mettle, Hearthen, Kindrith, Fyren) — Kindrith was the only one clear across all four axes (App Store, USPTO, domains, socials). Both `kindrith.com` and `kindrith.app` were genuinely unregistered at time of check. Code, Unity `productName`, and docs have been updated. The GitHub repo rename (`Pradeep-SG/ember` → `Pradeep-SG/kindrith`) and the filesystem directory rename are deferred to Pradeep. The body of this report below is kept intact as the historical record of why we renamed.

---

**Verdict: RENAME.** The name "Ember" is effectively blocked across every axis that matters for a consumer iOS wellness/game app in 2026. This is not a close call.

Research performed 2026-04-24. Sources at the bottom.

**Apple will likely not let us reuse the bare "Ember" name even if we tried.** Even if they did, ASO is hopeless — we'd be the 15th+ search result.

A compound mark ("EMBER HABIT FORGE") helps marginally but doesn't clear the pending Amazon block.

## Overall verdict

Rename.

- **App Store** saturation means no discoverability for the bare word "Ember."
- **Amazon's pending TM in 009/041/042/044** is a direct block on every software class we'd need; Delaware North and Light & Wonder cover games; Cercacor covers health monitoring.
- **All four candidate domains** are held; playember.com is held by a funded competing game studio.
- **All short social handles** are taken.

A qualifier ("Ember: Habit Forge") would not help meaningfully — it doesn't clear Amazon's pending mark, doesn't win ASO against 15 existing "Ember" habit/health apps, and doesn't free up any of the domains. Distinctive single-word names (the "Kindle" pattern — a word that didn't mean anything adjacent to the product category until the product arrived) save tens of thousands in TM fights and years of ASO disadvantage.
